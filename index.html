Ironing Calibration Converter

For the Customizable Ironing Calibration on MakerWorld. Drop in your 3MF and get it back with every pad's ironing flow and speed already set.

1. Drop your 3MF

Your file stays on your computer. Nothing is uploaded.

2. Check your pads

Seen from above, with the front of the plate at the bottom.

3. Save the new 3MF

Advanced settings
  • Ironing type: Topmost surface only, so the label strips aren't ironed.
  • Pattern: Rectilinear.
  • Fixed ironing angle: on, so every pad is ironed in the same direction.

These apply to every pad. Use the same values when you set up ironing with your result.

4. Slice and print

  1. Open the new 3MF and pick your printer, filament and process as usual.
  2. Turn off ironing overrides in your filament settings (Setting Overrides → Ironing). If they're ticked, every pad irons the same.
  3. Slice and print, then pick the smoothest pad. Its flow and speed are engraved on it.

How it works: each pad has a tiny code on its underside that holds its flow and speed. It's only 0.02 mm deep, too thin to print, but this page can read it.
